People always buy three things: certainty, speed, or cover.

No one wants your expertise.

Certainty stops the guessing.

Speed gets them to their goal this year instead of next.

Cover means they're not the only one carrying the decision.

And this is where most founders get stuck. They think they're selling a strategy,
a service, a package, their experience.

But nobody is paying for your experience. They're paying to stop being the only one
who has to be right.

Someone with a plan so they don't have to invent it.

Someone who can explain where the number came from.

So the question isn't "Is my offer good?" The question is "What is my client afraid
of getting wrong?"

Because when they can't see who's carrying the risk, they go quiet. But when your offer
makes it obvious you're carrying it with them, selling stops feeling like persuading.

**Which of the three is your offer actually selling? Tell me below.**
