## DELIVERY CUES
"this one was meant for you." - flat, certain. No hype. 0.5s.
"And you know what it is." - direct to camera. Hold eye contact. 0.5s.
"It's not your service. It's not your team." - two quick beats, then slower:
"It's how clients actually come in."
"referrals, a bit of outreach, and hoping" - dry. No contempt. Most viewers do this.
"You just get used to it." - FLAT. Silence either side. Emotional hinge of the reel.
"honestly, I don't know how." - warm, almost amused. Must sound like a genuine
thought, not a pitch. If this line sounds salesy the whole reel collapses.
"two hundred and forty thousand dollars in three months" - say it plainly, no
emphasis. Understating a real number reads as true.
"I'm taking three firms this month." - say it as a limitation, not a flex.
"that's genuinely all we can build properly" - underplay completely. This is what
makes the scarcity believable rather than fake urgency.
"So stop waiting for a quieter month. There isn't one." - 0.5s after.
CTA - calm, practical, no urgency voice. The risk-removal half is the important
part; deliver it like you mean it, because you have to honour it on the call.
~145 wpm. Voice only, no music. Static camera, one take.
