**LYLA:** Why do clients pay you more than they'd pay someone else for the same work?

**WAJAHAT:** Because they're not comparing me to someone else.

That's the whole thing. Price is not set by what the work costs. It's set by what
their other option costs them.

If their other option is a freelancer down the road, I'm expensive.

If their other option is hiring two people, paying salaries, training them, and
waiting six months to see if it works — I'm cheap.

The work is still the same. Only the number changed.

So when somebody tells you your rate is high, the real question is not whether
you're good enough.

It's who they're comparing you to.

And changing that is harder than changing your price.

Because what you're actually asking for is trust. And nobody decides that on the
call. They decide it before they ever speak to you — from what they find, and how
it looks when they find it.

That's not charm. That's identity.

You don't become expensive by raising your price.

You become the kind of firm that charges it.
