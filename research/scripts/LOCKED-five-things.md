Five things make a million dollar business. Four of them you could do right now.

Number one is one niche. Most people say they help small businesses. That is not a niche.
A dentist is. A law firm is. A consultation business is.

Number two is one service. This is the thing you are best at. If you get people clients,
get them clients. Do not build them a website as well.

Number three is one channel. This is where you find buyers. If that is Meta ads, run Meta
ads. Stop doing cold email and LinkedIn in the same month.

Number four is one tool. This is how you sell. It might be a call. It might be a DM. Pick
one and get good at it.

Number five is 360 days. You do those four things for 360 days and you change nothing.

That last one is the whole business.

Nobody fails at picking a niche. They fail when things get boring.

---

## WRITING RULE THIS SCRIPT FOLLOWS
Every sentence is complete and under about twelve words. "A dentist is." is three words and
a full sentence. "The one thing you're best at." is six words and is not. Short does not mean
fragmented. Each item follows the same three beats: name it, say what it is, give the instruction.

## WHAT MAKES IT OURS RATHER THAN HIS
He opens with "these are the only five steps" and ends with a guarantee that you will be a
millionaire. This one puts the twist in the hook instead, four of the five are easy, so the
whole reel is the viewer waiting to find out which one is hard. No guarantee is made.
