# Draft: the tier list reel
**Modelled on:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgNYbCXMzX4/ · "$100M CEO ranks your first investments"
· 26s, 7 items, single shot, off camera prompter, animated S to F graphic.

**Why that one worked:** S to F is a frame the viewer already understands at frame one, so there is
no setup. Every item resolves in about three seconds, so no stretch of the reel is dead. The spikes
come from the contrarian grades, and the agreeable grades are the flat bits that make the
contrarian ones land. People argue with rankings, so the comments write themselves.

**Header, persistent on screen:** Ranking where million dollar agencies spend money.

| # | Prompt | Grade | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A new website | **F** | Nobody ever signed because your website looked nice. |
| 2 | Meta ads | **S** | Nothing else puts your offer in front of strangers that fast. |
| 3 | AI writing your content | **F** | It writes things nobody would ever say out loud. |
| 4 | A CRM | **C** | A CRM will not follow up. It just remembers that you did not. |
| 5 | Hiring a salesperson | **D** | You just paid someone to call people who were never buying. |
| 6 | A form that asks for budget | **S** | Half your leads will disappear. That half was wasting your time. |
| 7 | Cold email | **C** | It is fine if you enjoy being ignored all day. |
| 8 | AI that answers a lead in the first minute | **S** | This is the only AI on the list that makes money. |
| 9 | More leads | **F** | You do not have a lead problem. You have a show up problem. |

**Closing line, to camera:**

> Most agencies spend everything on the F list. Then they ask me why the calendar is empty.

---

## Notes

**The AI pair is the smartest thing in it.** Item 3 grades AI F and item 8 grades AI S. Same
technology, opposite grades, and the difference is whether it touches a buyer. That reads as
judgement rather than a take, and it is the only place in the reel where Wajdan's actual thinking
shows without explaining anything.

**Grades alternate on purpose.** F, S, F, C, D, S, C, S, F. Never two uncomfortable grades in a
row. Reorder freely but keep that rule or the contrarian ones stop registering.

**Ends on the thesis.** Item 9 is the Wajdan position and it is an F, which hands straight into the
closing line about the F list.

**Shoot:** single shot at the desk, Danyal reads the prompts off camera, Wajahat answers. No
memorising, no transitions. Roughly twenty minutes. Tier graphic is added in the edit.

**Runtime:** about 40 seconds.

**Header alternative:** `$240K in 3 months. Ranking where agencies spend money.` Stronger authority.
Only usable with "3 months" visible, since the figure covers three months and not one.
