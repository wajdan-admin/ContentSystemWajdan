# Drafts: animated comparison reels
**Never locked, never shot.** Highest multiple format in the research: @candicemuna 2,504x,
@landflippingsimplified 2,165x, @strange.odds 1,754x, @pathmorph 721x from 456 followers.
No face, no actor, no recording. Travels identically in the US and Pakistan.

**Shared spec:** flat stick figures, two flat colours, white background, deliberately crude.
Upbeat music from frame one, no voiceover. Counters must tick, not cut. Time markers punch in
centre screen, large, 0.4s. Final text holds 3 to 4 seconds for a clean loop. Under 25 seconds.
Build the template once, every later one is a reskin.

**Numbers are a parable, like the coffee stand example. Keep them round so they read as a model.**

---
