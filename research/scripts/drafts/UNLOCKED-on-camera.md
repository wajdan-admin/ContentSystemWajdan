## 1. Objection roleplay: "Just send me a proposal" (~70s)
Model: @mikecesaroni, 2.3M views, 212x his median. One static shot, phone, no edit.
The hook IS the objection, said in the client's voice, then he answers in his own.

**[AS THE CLIENT, flat and polite]**
"Just send me a proposal and I'll have a look."

**[OWN VOICE]**
That's a no.
Not always. But most of the time, that's a no in a nicer jacket.
Think about what they just did. They took the decision out of the room and put it on paper,
where they don't have to say it to your face.
And you know what happens next. The proposal goes in a folder. They read the price. They don't
read the rest. And three weeks later you're writing the follow up that starts with just circling back.
So here's what to do instead.
You don't refuse. You just don't leave yet.
"Happy to. Before I write it, what would need to be in there for you to say yes?"
If they can answer that, you've got a buyer, and now you know exactly what to put in it.
If they can't, you just saved yourself four hours.

Other objections ready to shoot the same way: "We tried ads, leads came, nobody bought."
"A two minute form will kill my conversions." (a real comment on the 15.2K reel)
"Can you guarantee results?"

## 2. If I had to start from scratch (~50s)
Model: @aymen_berrahma, 688K views, 9,409 comments, 1.37% comment rate.
CTA is step four of the framework, not bolted on the end. Proof comes after the ask, as the reward.

Not to flex, but I'm quite good at getting consultancies clients.
If I had to start one from scratch tomorrow, here's exactly what I'd do.
One. Pick one kind of business to go after. Not the one that needs help the most. The one already
paying somebody else for this, and not happy about it.
Two. Read a hundred bad reviews of the people already serving them. Every complaint in there is a
line you can use.
Three. Say what you do as a number and a time. Not "we help firms grow." Something like thirty
booked calls in ninety days.
Four. Comment SCRATCH and I'll send you the page I'd build on day one. And I'll show you how I
went from a hundred dollars a month to doing this for eighty firms.

Alternative opening, stronger because it is not hypothetical:
"Four years ago I was making a hundred dollars a month. If I had to start a consultancy from
scratch tomorrow, here's exactly what I'd do."

## 3. The hiring analogy (~60s)
Model: @funsystems.io, 789 followers, 724K views, 401x median.

You posted a job and got four hundred applications.
Did you interview four hundred people?
Obviously not. You read the CVs. You binned most of them. You phoned maybe twelve. You interviewed four.
Nobody thinks that's rude. That's just hiring.
Now look at how you handle leads.
Four hundred enquiries come in.
And you let every single one of them book straight into your calendar.
No CV screen. No phone screen. Straight to the interview.
Then you sit in back to back calls with people who have no budget and no timeline, and you call it
a lead problem.
It isn't a lead problem.
You hired a recruiter for your jobs and nobody for your customers.
