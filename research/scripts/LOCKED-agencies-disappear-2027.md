## FINAL SCRIPT

The agencies that disappear in 2027 will have one thing in common.

They were good at the work.

That's it.

For twenty years that was enough. You could build a whole business on being better than
the other agency, because the client couldn't tell the difference until they have worked
with you.

That's not true anymore.

And it's not because AI does the work better.

Because it made good enough cheap.

And good enough is what most of them were buying.

So the agencies that survive won't be the best ones.

They'll be the ones who own how clients find them.

Who've got a way to get in front of someone that doesn't depend on a referral, or getting
lucky on a platform.

And here's the worst part.

Being good at the work is the thing they're proudest of.

That's why they'll be the last ones to accept it.
