## The spec

|---|---|
| **Length** | **Organic education 60–120s. Paid/promo 20–45s.** Corrected 2026-11-25 — length was never the problem |
| **Hook** | Frame one. Speech or text at 0:00. No logo, no intro, no music ramp |

| **Language** | English, US/international standard |
| **Cadence** | 4–5/week, never two of the same concept in one week |

## Scoring: post it or kill it
1. Is every section earning its time? (organic 60–120s, promo 20–45s)
2. Does the hook land at 0:00 with no wind-up?
3. Is the first line about **them**, not Wajdan?
4. Does it survive without the CTA — real value either way?
5. Is it a format from the library, not a new experiment?
6. Does it avoid being a pitch? (house non-selly rule)
7. Can it be shot in under an hour?

**5 of 7 = post. Under 5 = rework.**

## Hard rules
- Never invent performance numbers. Real named case studies are allowed; invented ones never.
- Never two near-identical concepts in the same week (cost 5.9K vs 564 on 8 Nov).
- Milestones and hiring go to Stories, never the grid.
- The body never sells. The ask lives in the final line, end card, and caption only.
