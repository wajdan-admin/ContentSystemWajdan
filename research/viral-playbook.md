# Wajdan — Viral Format Playbook
**Built 2026-11-25.** Every format below is backed by a post that outperformed its own
creator's median by a large multiple. Multiple = views / that account's median.

**Creator list lives in `creators.md`.** This file is formats only.

**How to use:** pick a format, pick a topic from your list, pick a hook. One format x many
topics = a series. One topic x many formats = repurposing. That grid is the content engine.

---

## FORMAT 1 — Split-screen stick-figure comparison  ** HIGHEST MULTIPLES, NO FACE **

**Proof:** @candicemuna 2,504x median from 3.3K followers. @landflippingsimplified 15M views, 2,165x. @strange.odds 17.5M, 1,754x. @pathmorph 4M from 456 followers. Links and five more in `creators.md`.

**Why it fits Wajdan:** no face, no voice, no accent. Works identically in the US, UAE and
Pakistan. Account size is nearly irrelevant - 456 followers did 4M.

**Build:** qualified vs unqualified leads - cheap leads vs expensive clients - 18-second
reply vs 2-day reply - open calendar vs gated calendar - leads bought vs leads earned -
DIY stack vs one system - founder taking calls vs team taking calls.

**Hooks (on-screen text, nothing spoken):**
- `SAME $2,000 AD SPEND`
- `100 LEADS  |  10 CALLS`
- `A FULL CALENDAR ISN'T A GOOD CALENDAR`
- `CHEAP LEADS. EXPENSIVE CLIENTS.`
- `SAME SPEND. SAME MONTH.`
- `YOU DIDN'T SAVE MONEY. YOU SPENT YOUR WEEK.`

**Reuse:** build the template once. Every future one is a reskin - swap the two things being
compared and the counters. 20-25s, music only, looping, deliberately crude art.

## FORMAT 2 — Named numbered rule  ** SINGLE HIGHEST MULTIPLE FOUND **

**Proof:** @uncensoredcmo, 3M views at 4,442x their median. The highest multiple in the whole dataset. Link in `creators.md`.

**Critical detail:** the 4,442x one was a rule about **how people think**, not about a product.
Rules about human behaviour travel. Rules about your service do not.

**Build:** rules about buyer behaviour, not about your system.

**Hooks:**
- "The 5-30-60 rule." (how long before an enquiry stops being yours)
- "The rule of four forms." (a serious buyer contacted four people, not one)
- "The two-minute rule." (what a buyer decides before they speak to anyone)
- "The agreement rule." (the ones who agree with everything never buy)

**Reuse:** each rule is one episode. The naming convention itself becomes recognisable - after
five, people know what a "Wajdan rule" looks like.

## FORMAT 3 — Objection roleplay, single shot  ** ZERO PRODUCTION **

**Proof:** @mikecesaroni, 2.3M views at 212x his median. One static shot, no edit. Link in `creators.md`.

One static shot. He acts out an angry client phone call. Hook = the client's complaint, verbatim.

**Build:** every objection you have ever been hit with on a sales call.

**Hooks (say the objection, do not describe it):**
- "'Why are you more expensive than the agency down the road?'"
- "'We tried ads. Leads came. Nobody bought.'"
- "'Just send me a proposal and I'll have a look.'"
- "'A two-minute form will kill my conversions.'"  <- real comment on your own 15.2K reel
- "'Can you guarantee results?'"
- "'We'll start after Ramadan / after Q1 / after the new site.'"

**Reuse:** your CRM and your comment section are an infinite episode list. One objection = one
reel. This is the cheapest format you have - phone, one take, no edit.

## FORMAT 4 — "I don't gatekeep" authority walkthrough

**Proof:** @lukegass_, 560K views at 478x his median, from 4,900 followers. Link in `creators.md`.

Authority claim -> real client result -> "I'm not gatekeeping" -> walk the whole thing on screen.

**Build:** already scripted. One per layer, or one per client vertical.

**Hooks:**
- "I run a client acquisition firm in Budapest. Here's the whole system, free."
- "We did this for a corporate services group. Here's exactly how."
- "Eighty firms. Same six steps. I'll show you all of them."

**Reuse:** one per layer of the system = six reels from one shoot day.

## FORMAT 5 — Analogy explainer

**Proof:** @funsystems.io, 724K views at 401x their median, from 789 followers. Link in `creators.md`.

**Build:** map an abstract business idea onto something everyone has lived.

**Hooks:**
- "You'd never interview all four hundred applicants."       (hiring = qualification)
- "Nobody walks into A&E and goes straight to surgery."      (triage = screening)
- "You wouldn't let a stranger book your surgeon."           (gated calendar)
- "A restaurant with no reservations isn't busy. It's chaos." (open calendar)

**Reuse:** one analogy per concept. Each one is reusable as an ad hook later.

## FORMAT 7 — Contrarian prediction, zero production

**Proof:** @second___harvest, 1.6M views at 426x their median. Filmed in a car, one static text line. Link in `creators.md`.

Filmed in a car. One long static text line. Background music. That is the entire production.

**Hooks:**
- "The agencies that disappear in 2027 will all have the same thing in common."
- "In two years nobody will be selling 'leads'."
- "Local pricing is a choice, not a market."
- "The next wave of consultancies won't have salespeople."

**Reuse:** one per quarter. Over-use kills it - prediction content needs scarcity.

## FORMAT 8 — "These are exactly the same"

**Proof:** @create.with.chris04, 1M views at 1,996x their median, from 5,100 followers. Link in `creators.md`.

Identical inputs, wildly different outputs. The surprise IS the hook.

**Build:** two landing pages - two ads with the same budget - two follow-up sequences -
two sales calls.

**Hooks:**
- "Same ad. Same budget. Same offer. Only one of these books calls."
- "These two pages cost the same to build."
- "Same lead. Two businesses. One of them closed it."

**Reuse:** pairs with Format 1. Same logic, live screen recording instead of animation.

**~35 live cells. At 4 posts a week that is two months of content from eight templates.**

## Three rules that hold across every winner
1. **The hook is on screen at 0:00.** No logo, no intro, no music ramp.
2. **Production value is inversely correlated with performance.** Stick figures, cars,
   single takes and phone footage beat studio work in every comparison in this dataset.
3. **Subject beats mechanism.** Money, pricing, buyer psychology and identity outperform
   system explainers by roughly 10x median multiple in this niche.
