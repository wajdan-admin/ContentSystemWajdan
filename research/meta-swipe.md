- "Roofers: 75 Qualified Appointments in 90 Days" — Roof Lead Accelerator
- "30 Remodeling Clients. 90 Days. Guaranteed." — Kitchen & Bath Marketing Company
- "Chiropractors Doing $50k+/m" — Chiro Jump

## WARNING: everyone niches by vertical, nobody says "consultancies"

Nobody found targeting "consultancies" or "law firms." That is **both a gap and a warning** — the winners niche HARD and name the vertical in the headline. "Consultation firms" may be too abstract to carry an ad headline.
